# IdeaGeneration

A curated collection of high-growth SaaS product ideas with market analysis, traffic potential, and monetization strategies.

## Contents

- [High-Growth SaaS Product Ideas](./SAAS_IDEAS.md) — Detailed breakdown of SaaS niches with strong revenue and growth potential
- [What to Build & How to Earn Money Operating a SaaS](./BUILD_AND_OPERATE.md) — Actionable guide: choosing what to build, shipping an MVP, setting up payments, launching, and growing to $10K+ MRR

## Purpose

This repository is a living document for brainstorming and evaluating SaaS business opportunities, and for providing practical guidance on how to build and operate them for real income. Each idea is assessed across:

- 📈 **Growth potential** — market size and trajectory
- 🚦 **Traffic potential** — organic and paid acquisition channels
- 💰 **Revenue model** — pricing, ARPU, and monetization paths
- 🔨 **Build guidance** — what to build first, MVP scope, tech stack, and launch strategy
